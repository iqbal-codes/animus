## Constraints before an authorized design or run

- Freeze and validate parent membership, directed dependencies and child contracts; hold cycles, missing or ambiguous members. Factory admission requires closed dependencies, and its workflow contract requires outcomes on the chosen base, not merely a closed issue ([contract](https://github.com/iqbal-codes/labq-software-factory/blob/5fa30a37748d0acde86a6fc6c0fc3ba49d5e8105/docs/agents/software-factory-workflow.md#L57-L90)). Any policy for integrating still-open dependent children needs explicit approval rather than an intake bypass.
- Assign ownership of child selection, isolated checkouts, integration branch serialization, conflict handling, retries/recovery and stale graph/base revalidation. Per-item claims do not establish a parent-wide branch lock. Whether an external coordinator is a permitted thin consumer rather than a duplicate scheduler remains undecided.
- Bind final PR head H, destination base B and tested merge tree or runtime identity M. Require independently generated review and runtime evidence for the full contract and a trustworthy M-to-running-target mapping. Hold absent, stale, inconclusive, mismatched or malformed evidence; re-evaluate on head/base changes. Runtime scenario secrecy, environment ownership and teardown must be established separately.
- Explicitly decide whether one final PR means one PR total; child PRs to an integration branch are not that. PR creation, review publication, deployment, approval and merging are distinct authorizations, not consequences of catalog presence or issue research. Resolve branch protection, required checks, protected paths and fresh forge readback before merge.

[INFERENCE] Serializing approved child implementations without child PRs could be a candidate design using the implement primitive, but neither supported invocation in the configured host nor the necessary coordination and evidence bindings have been verified. An upstream-supported extension or a human-owned sequence may be alternatives; neither is established as satisfying the issue.
